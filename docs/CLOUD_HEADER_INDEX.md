# Partial offline cloud-header index

When live IRSA search fails, the application can use small, checksummed indices of SPHEREx parent-image headers checked in NASA's public S3 mirror. They are deliberately **partial**, are not live search results, and do not establish the identity or motion of any target. A parent image's WCS covered the indexed coordinate at verification time; image access, cutout coverage and data quality are checked separately.

The Epsilon Indi index examines 289 objects from 12 narrowly selected S3 prefixes near previously identified observations. Six detector images cover the current preset coordinate with WCS inverse/forward round-trip residual below 0.01 arcsecond and sufficient distance from the detector edge. One initially plausible inverse projection failed the round trip and was excluded. These six image records plus the two existing cached demo records yield eight observations when both releases are searched; several images were taken close together and are not eight independent epochs.

The WISE 0855 index begins with a time-spaced sample of 72 unique image URLs from a previous IRSA search near an earlier WISE coordinate. All 72 corresponding S3 FITS headers were independently checked at the **current** preset coordinate and passed the same WCS round-trip and edge checks. Search results are capped at 60 and preserve the two existing cached demonstration images. The sample is not a complete search and does not establish a WISE 0855 detection.

The two indices are restricted to within 0.00015 degrees of their indexed coordinates, filtered by release and detector, and excluded in cache-only demo mode. Their observation dates are taken from `MJD-BEG` in each parent FITS image header. Stored ETags identify the objects examined during the survey; they are not a guarantee that the object is unchanged at a later access. Cloud cutout retrieval independently validates consistent object identity during each read, checks the observation and detector in its FITS header, and falls back to the IRSA cutout service if needed.

Each compressed index has a checksum-bearing `_sources.json` sidecar and a compressed `_evidence.json.gz` survey record in `data/manifests`. `scripts/package_cloud_header_survey.py` packages a previously saved, attributed survey and refuses candidate footprints without the WCS round-trip check. The indices are intended as outage assistance, not a replacement for live IRSA SIA/TAP searches or an automatically refreshed archive index.
