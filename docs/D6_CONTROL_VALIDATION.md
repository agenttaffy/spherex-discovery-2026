# D6 ePSF real-source controls

## Selection and acceptance rules

Use wider D6 cutouts of the two staged WISE 0855 exposures and the two staged Epsilon Indi B exposures. These are two sky fields and four exposure IDs, including DR1 and QR3. Use the same catalog neighborhood for both epochs of each field. Keep the named systems outside the control sample. Assess all eligible catalog matches without screening on a fitted SPHEREx amplitude.

The repeatability extension uses the previously documented compatible deep-field DR1 pair at RA 270°, Dec +66.56°. The pair was chosen from prior image-level bandpass verification, with no selection on source-fit behavior. It uses the same rules and a third catalog neighborhood. [Earlier pair provenance and verification](../TEST_RESULTS.md)

- Require a Gaia DR3 proper-motion solution, RUWE ≤1.4 and a finite nonnegative parallax ≤20 mas to bound the omitted observer parallax to a small fraction of a native pixel. Reject Gaia sources flagged as variable, duplicated or non-single. Project the Gaia J2016.0 coordinates to the cutout's UTC `MJD-AVG` using the two proper-motion components. For the AllWISE match, propagate to MJD 55400.0 (J2010.5589) and require a mutual nearest match within 1″ to the catalog's motion-fit coordinates. Stationary-fit positions are retained in the neighbor screen when motion-fit positions are missing. Use the Gaia prediction for all SPHEREx fits. [Gaia astrometric solution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03380) · [AllWISE catalog column definitions](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/WISE/docs/release/AllWISE/expsup/sec2_1a.html)
- Require a measured AllWISE profile W2 magnitude with uncertainty ≤0.15 mag, no W2 artifact flag or saturation, point-like morphology and no deblend where the catalog exposes those fields. Reject any other Gaia or AllWISE neighbor within the 4.5-pixel fitting footprint, with a half-pixel margin for astrometric and catalog incompleteness. A Gaia/AllWISE pair provides independent positional and infrared selection. It supplies an absolute D6 reference only if a separately measured spectrum and matched spectral response support synthetic photometry.
- Retain the prototype's strict flags, complete 2.5-pixel core, contained footprint, source-excluded background and stable design. For a positional recovery require formal S/N ≥5, χ² tail probability ≥0.01, all four ±0.2-pixel axis perturbations, and less than or equal to 10% amplitude change under either centroid or background perturbation. The formal statistics alone do not calibrate the uncertainty.
- For null tests use a fixed disjoint 10-pixel grid. Require complete catalog coverage of the fitting footprint, no Gaia or AllWISE source within 5.5 pixels and no SOURCE flags in the fit footprint. Use at most one observation per sky field for the blank statistics. The pre-existing gates require at least 20 independent blank positions and 20 matched spectral references. For repeated measurements require the same clean positional recovery at both epochs and at least 90% local passband overlap. A single pair cannot establish empirical uncertainty calibration. [Original study criteria](TARGET_VALIDATION.md#forced-fit-study-acceptance-criteria)

## Reproduce locally

From the repository root, run `python scripts/validate_d6_controls.py --fetch` once to stage public cutouts and catalog queries. Subsequent runs omit `--fetch` and use the saved inputs. The script reads the D6 SAPM and CWAVE/CBAND files from the prior study and writes `data/cache/target_validation/epsf_study/real_controls/real_source_controls.json`. The report includes exact catalog URLs, FITS SHA-256 checksums, exposure midpoints, and per-source rejection reasons. For the interpolation check, install `photutils==2.3.0` in an isolated environment that has this project's dependencies and run with `--photutils-check`.

Catalog queries are 460″ cones centered on the pair's field. Neighbor checks use every returned catalog position. Gaia positions with unknown proper motion and unmatched AllWISE neighbors use their available catalog positions for this screen. This limits the isolation test for faint, missing or fast-moving neighbors. Require the test position to be at least 36″ from the catalog query edge and, for the challenge fields, more than seven native pixels from the named prediction.

## Product and convention checks

- Evaluated five detector zones at four integer or fractional phases in each of the six FITS files. All 120 normalized signed native stamps had zero maximum per-pixel difference from the IRSA notebook's `ImagePSF.evaluate` and native-stamp renormalization. This checks the interpolation and normalization convention. It leaves the true source response and absolute photometric scaling unvalidated. [IRSA ePSF tutorial, §7.2](https://caltech-ipac.github.io/irsa-tutorials/spherex-psf/#72-using-the-spherex-epsf-in-forward-modeling-eg-tractor)
- The release D6 area map has the expected checksum and sits in the same detector coordinates as the cutouts. Area variation across each tested cutout ranged from 0.071% to 0.138% of its minimum area. The prototype converts the unit-flux model pixel by pixel. The supplement describes converting Tractor amplitudes with the local solid angle. There was no eligible real-source fit for a direct comparison of the two conventions or for exposure-specific area verification. The supplement's empirical 3% catalog term comes from repeated stellar measurements and is separate from the Level 2 variance plane. The prototype lacks the mission's global background subtraction and multi-source scene fit. The mission core fit-quality statistic is mean absolute standardized residual over 2.5 pixels. The prototype also reports nominal χ² over its selected fitting pixels. [SPHEREx Explanatory Supplement, §3.3](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/SPHEREx/docs/SPHEREx_Expsupp.pdf)
- AllWISE W2 is broadband near 4.6 µm. One W2 magnitude cannot specify an isolated star's narrow D6 flux. A physical cross-calibration requires a separately measured spectrum, W2 response and zero point, and the response appropriate to the source's SPHEREx detector position. The public SPHEREx response package offers representative lab curves and detector maps of band centers and widths. CWAVE/CBAND support nominal overlap checks and give no complete transmission curve by themselves. No matched reference spectra or defensible color correction were established for this sample. [AllWISE photometry guidance](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/WISE/docs/release/AllWISE/faq.html) · [SPHEREx spectral calibration products](https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/SPHEREx/docs/spherex_spectral_calibrations.html)

There were 578 catalog-quality star-exposure applications across the six images. The footprint excluded 316, the catalog-boundary or named-target exclusion removed five, and catalog neighbors excluded the other 257. No real-source fits passed the pre-fit selection. Of 225 disjoint blank-grid positions, 219 had catalog neighbors, three hit the catalog-boundary or named-target exclusion, two had SOURCE flags and one had an invalid or masked core.

The earlier positional recovery, Gaia DR3 `5756943312809452160` / AllWISE `J085511.95−071854.0`, has a 0.039″ epoch-adjusted match and W2 `13.468 ± 0.032` mag. It passed catalog quality here. A second catalog source within 3.76 native pixels placed it inside the exclusion zone on both WISE exposures. The earlier formal S/N 6.00 and reduced χ² 1.39 remain experimental diagnostics of a less isolated scene. No matched reference flux or independent repeat was available.

The deep field clears the nominal bandpass gate at the field center, using the smallest overlap over both 2.5-pixel cores. This measures spectral compatibility, and the earlier image-pair test used aligned pixels instead. None of the three pairs has two clean recoveries of a common catalog star. There are **zero** absolute-reference residuals, repeat residuals and accepted null pulls. Formal uncertainty calibration is therefore unestablished. The 28 earlier injections into catalog-contaminated patches test algebra and perturbations only, with a normalized scatter of 0.608 in each S/N bin. They do not supply real-source or null calibration. [Earlier injection results](TARGET_VALIDATION.md#control-results)

**No-go for calibrated D6 source photometry in this sample.** Future validation needs fields selected in advance for both isolation and independently measured infrared spectra, reusable local response provenance, and a set of same-source matched-passband repeats. For crowded fields it would also need a calibrated joint scene model. The product retains unavailable target fluxes and field-sample-only diagnostics.
