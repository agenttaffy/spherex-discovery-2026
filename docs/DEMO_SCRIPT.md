# Judging Demo Script (2-Minute Presentation Guide)

## Act 1: The Problem & The Mission (0:00 - 0:30)
- "NASA's SPHEREx mission is mapping the entire sky in 102 infrared wavelengths every 6 months. But raw Level 2 FITS files are 70 MB each and locked in complex archives. How can citizen scientists and astronomers spot moving asteroids, brown dwarfs, or variable stars across epochs?"
- "Introducing **SPHEREx Discovery** — an interactive web observatory bringing NASA's dynamic infrared universe to your browser."

## Act 2: Live Coordinates & Time-Lapse Blink (0:30 - 1:15)
- Open the application.
- Click **Barnard's Star** preset (RA: 269.452°, Dec: +4.693°).
- Show the observation timeline spanning DR1 (August 2025) and QR3 (August 2026).
- Hit **Play** or enable **Blink Comparison**:
  - Point out the visible 1.6-pixel astrometric shift of Barnard's Star over the 1-year baseline (10.3″/yr proper motion).
- Switch to **RW Aurigae** preset:
  - Demonstrate photometric variability where the classical T Tauri star fluctuates in infrared surface brightness.

## Act 3: Scientific Rigor & Provenance (1:15 - 1:45)
- Point to the right telemetry panel:
  - Explain the 6 detector bands (D1–D6), calibrated units in MegaJanskys per steradian (MJy/sr), and per-pixel data quality flags.
  - Show the direct NASA/IPAC IRSA provenance link to the exact Level 2 FITS file.
  - Toggle contrast stretching between `asinh` and `ZScale` to highlight faint background nebular structure.

## Act 4: The Vision (1:45 - 2:00)
- "SPHEREx Discovery turns gigabytes of complex mission data into immediate, discoverable science. Thank you!"
